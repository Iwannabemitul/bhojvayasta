How BhojVayasta Works (In 3 Steps)

From kitchen surplus to a hot meal for someone in need.

01. Report Extra Food

Confirm Food is Safe & Packaged

Kitchens and banquet halls enter how many meals they have, when it was cooked, and verify it is hot (>60°C) and sealed cleanly.

02. Nearby Pickup

Pickup on the Live Map

Nearby volunteers see the food alert on their map with driving distance. One click claims the pickup and sets the route.

03. Delivery to Shelters

Delivered to Children & Elders

The volunteer delivers directly to a verified orphanage or slum relief camp. The meals are logged to the community dashboard.
